# Data Rights and Attribution

The analytical CSV files in this directory are derived from public records of
the U.S. Environmental Protection Agency, U.S. Energy Information
Administration, U.S. Department of the Treasury, and U.S. Bureau of Economic
Analysis. The files are distributed to permit verification and replication of
the reported research.

The repository's MIT License applies to the programs and does not assign a new
license to third-party source records. Rights, attribution requirements, and
reuse conditions associated with those records remain with the originating
agencies and any other applicable source providers. Users planning uses beyond
verification or replication should consult the source pages identified in
[`README.md`](README.md).

When using the analytical files, cite the research paper, identify the
repository version, and cite the originating agencies appropriate to the data
components used.
